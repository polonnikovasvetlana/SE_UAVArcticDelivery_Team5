# Medicine dispatch candidates: rapid screen

Checked: 5 November 2026. Scope: a brief evidence screen using the existing 57 candidates, not an exhaustive facility inventory.

Map copy: [Arctic_relay_map_1_medical.png](Arctic_relay_map_1_medical.png), [SVG](Arctic_relay_map_1_medical.svg). Source map `Arctic_relay_map_1` is preserved.

Green rings identify documented medicine dispensing services. Staff capability is inferred from the medical/pharmacy service, not a verified current roster. Amber rings identify medical leads or older/indirect medicine evidence. Unringed points are unverified, not evidence of no medical service. Exact drug availability, cold storage, opening hours, access and UAV dispatch agreements remain unverified at every location.

**Result:** Main ports are useful starting points, but the shortlist also includes Naryan-Mar, Egvekinot, Bilibino and inland Norilsk. Port status alone does not establish medicine availability. Tiksi, Khatanga and Dikson should remain priority follow-up candidates, rather than being rejected because the quickly retrieved evidence is older.

Counts: 8 documented medicine-service candidates; 12 medical/historical leads; 37 not verified. Only the first two groups received specific source leads; the remaining candidates were retained without individual verification within the time limit.
